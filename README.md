Velvet Rope — The Meridian Gallery Heist: a top-down 2D canvas heist game. Steal the Aurora Diamond from the gallery vault, dodge four patrolling guards and their vision cones, slip past two sweeping security cameras, and reach the getaway exit — while the alarm meter decides whether you walk out a ghost or get caught. Decoy coins, an EMP burst, three routes through the gallery, and a lockpick that only works while no cone is on you.
